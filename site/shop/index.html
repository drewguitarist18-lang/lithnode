Questions

What do I need?

Lithnode (free, Windows, Mac or Linux) and a Claude account with Claude Code. The Pro plan is enough.

Does it cost anything per run?

No. Runs use your own Claude plan. Each team uses 5 or 6 agents on Sonnet and Haiku, never Opus, so a run is light.

Can it buy things or sign me up?

No. The teams search and read the web and write their plan into a file. They never buy, sign up or contact anyone. You act on what they find.

Why pay when I could build a team myself?

You can, and Lithnode is free and open source. These save you the tuning: each team was run for real, its prompts fixed where it went wrong, and its checks set to cut bad results.

Reply to your receipt email and say what happened, with what you typed. You'll get help getting it working.
